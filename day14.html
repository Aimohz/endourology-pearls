1. Intrarenal Pressure & Translocation

  • 30 mm Hg (~40 cm H₂O) Ceiling

    Exceeding 30 mm Hg triggers pyelovenous/pyelotubular backflow; hand pumps without egress easily spike IRP to 60–100+ mm Hg.

2. Microbiological Discrepancy

  • Stone Culture Gold Standard

    In 20%–30% of patients with negative pre-op voided bladder cultures, renal pelvic urine or crushed stone cultures are positive. Send both in high-risk cases.

3. Risk Factors & The 60–90 Minute Rule

  • Time Thresholds

    Sepsis rises steeply past 60 min for URS and 90 min for PCNL. Stage the procedure if clearance is incomplete.

4. Recognition & Hour-1 Resuscitation

  • SIRS, qSOFA & PCT

    qSOFA ≥ 2 mandates immediate ICU escalation; Procalcitonin rises within 2–4 h.

  • Bundle & Source Control

    Draw lactate & 2 blood cultures, give broad-spectrum IV antipseudomonal/ESBL coverage (Meropenem or Pip-Tazo ± Amikacin), 30 mL/kg crystalloid, Norepinephrine if MAP < 65, and immediately verify stent/PCN patency.
