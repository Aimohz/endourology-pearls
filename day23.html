1. Trapped Basket Crisis Protocol

  • Never Pull Forcefully

    Forceful traction intussuscepts and avulses the ureter. Instead: 1) Disassemble basket handle & remove outer sheath; 2) Pass small scope alongside wire; 3) Laser the stone through the basket mesh; or 4) Cut distal wires.

2. Minor vs. Major Perforations

  • Management

    Minor (small contrast plume, wire in place): abort, place 6–7 Fr stent (2–4 wk) + Foley 24–48 h. Major (fat visible): 7–8 Fr stent (6–8 wk) if wire across; if access lost, place immediate PCN and attempt rendezvous.

3. Avulsion Reconstruction Hierarchy

  • By Level

    Distal (Grade I): ureteroneocystostomy + psoas hitch. Mid (Grade II): Boari flap + psoas hitch (bridges 10–12 cm) or TUU. Proximal (Grade III): ureterocalicostomy / nephropexy. Total (Grade IV): ileal ureter (contraindicated if Cr > 2.0 mg/dL) or renal autotransplantation.
