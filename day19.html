1. Gestational Renal Physiology

  • Hyperfiltration & Hydronephrosis

    GFR rises 30%–50% with absorptive hypercalciuria offset by hypercitraturia. Physiologic hydronephrosis occurs in up to 90% right / 67% left from progesterone and dextrorotated uterus.

2. Diagnostic Imaging

  • Ultrasound + Doppler

    Obstructive RI > 0.70 or ΔRI ≥ 0.04, absent unilateral ureteral jet, and transvaginal US for UVJ stones.

  • Second-Line

    Non-contrast MRI (HASTE) (no gadolinium) or ultra-low-dose CT (<5 mGy vs. 50–100 mGy deterministic teratogenic threshold).

3. Medical & Surgical Protocols

  • Analgesia

    70%–80% pass conservatively. Acetaminophen first-line; NSAIDs contraindicated in 3rd trimester (ductus closure/oligohydramnios).

  • Definitive URS vs. Stents

    Stents require 4–6 week exchanges due to rapid encrustation. Definitive Ho:YAG URS (safest in 2nd trimester, 15° left lateral tilt, fluoroless or pelvic lead shield, <10 W, OB fetal monitoring) is first-line.
