1. Molecular Pathophysiology & Diagnostics

  • COLA Defect

    Autosomal recessive SLC3A1 / SLC7A9 defect impairing proximal reabsorption of Cystine, Ornithine, Lysine, Arginine. Hexagonal crystals (20%–25%) and cherry-red nitroprusside test (>75 mg/L).

2. Biophysical Resistance & Medical Protocol

  • Surgical Reality

    SWL fails (<15%–25% SFR) and is contraindicated >10 mm; use high-energy Ho:YAG (1.0–1.5 J, 8–12 Hz) or TFL.

  • Hydration & Alkalinization

    Target urine output ≥ 3.5–4.0 L/d (concentration <250 mg/L), sodium <100–150 mEq/d, and potassium citrate (60–120 mEq/d) targeting pH 7.0–7.5 (never >7.5–7.8 to avoid CaP crusting).

3. Thiol Chelation & Brushite Parallel

  • Tiopronin

    800–1,500 mg/d forms a 50× more soluble mixed disulfide; check quarterly CBC and 24h urine protein (stop if >1.0 g/day).

  • Brushite (CaHPO₄·2H₂O)

    Forms at pH 6.2–6.8, attenuation >1,200–1,600 HU, SWL-resistant; manage with URS/PCNL + thiazides + low sodium.
