1. Fluid Dynamics & IRP Regulation

  • Outflow Conduit

    Matched sheath (e.g., 7.5–8.5 Fr scope in 11/13 Fr UAS) keeps resting IRP < 15–20 cm H₂O even during active irrigation, preventing pyelovenous backflow and dissipating laser heat (>20 W).

2. Ureteral Wall Injury: Traxer / PULS Grading

  • Grades 0–4

    Grade 0: none. Grade 1: mucosal erosion only. Grade 2: smooth muscle exposed. Grade 3: full-thickness tear exposing periureteral fat. Grade 4: avulsion.

  • Incidence

    Up to 45%–50% have some lesion (mostly Grade 1); significant Grade ≥ 2 injuries occur in 8%–13% of unstented ureters.

3. Radial Wall Stress & Stricture Risk

  • Microvascular Ischemia

    Oversized sheaths (12/14 or 14/16 Fr in primary ureters) compress adventitial vessels. Baseline post-URS stricture rate is ~1% but rises sharply with unstented Grade 2–3 tears.

4. Sizing & The No-Force Insertion Rule

  • Primary vs. Pre-Stented

    Favor 9.5/11.5 or 10/12 Fr in unstented ureters; 11/13 or 12/14 Fr fits smoothly after pre-stenting.

  • Resistance Threshold

    Advance over a stiff wire under fluoroscopy. If firm resistance is felt, stop immediately, place a 6 Fr stent, and stage RIRS in 7–14 days. Position sheath tip just below the UPJ—never inside the renal pelvis.

5. Mandatory Withdrawal Inspection & Stenting

  • PULS-Guided Stenting

    Inspect the entire ureteral lumen while withdrawing the sheath. Grade 0–1: omit or string stent 3–5 d. Grade 2: stent 2–3 wk. Grade 3: stent 4–6 wk + 3-month imaging.
