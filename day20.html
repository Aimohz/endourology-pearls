1. Ballistic / Pneumatic Lithotripsy

  • Tensile Spallation

    Compressed air drives a longitudinal stress wave (~5,000 m/s) that reflects as a tensile wave—stones are 4–10× weaker in tension. Zero heat, shatters hardest stones, but causes retropulsion and lacks suction.

2. Ultrasonic Lithotripsy

  • Piezoelectric Action

    Hollow probe vibrates at 23–27 kHz (30–100 µm) with integrated suction. Never run dry: stagnant activation exceeds 60–80 °C in <10 seconds, causing mucosal burns and strictures.

3. Single-Probe Dual-Energy Systems

  • Trilogy & ShockPulse-SE

    Combine ballistic impact + ultrasonic vibration + wide-bore suction in one probe, boosting clearance velocity from 15–20 mm³/s to >45–60 mm³/s.

  • Vacuum Collapse Guard

    High suction (300–500 mm Hg) without matched inflow collapses the renal pelvis onto the vibrating tip—release suction immediately if the field darkens.
