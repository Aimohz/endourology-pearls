1. Horseshoe Kidney Hazards

  • Anatomy & Retrorenal Colon

    90%–95% lower-pole fusion at L4–L5; anteriorly rotated pelvis with high ureteral insertion. Retrorenal colon incidence jumps to 5%–10%—cross-sectional CT and US-guided puncture are mandatory.

2. PCNL & RIRS in Horseshoe Kidneys

  • Upper-Pole PCNL Rule

    Lower calyces point medially over the spine; puncture an upper-pole posterior calyx (more medial, vertical, deeper—have 17–20 cm Amplatz sheaths ready).

  • RIRS Tactics

    Pre-stent 7–14 days to straighten high-riding UPJ loops; avoid forcing stiff UAS over the isthmus; relocate lower-pole stones before lasing.

3. Pelvic & Cross-Fused Ectopic Kidneys

  • Pelvic Ectopic Kidney

    Posterior flank puncture is impossible due to sacrum/iliac vessels/bowel. Use RIRS (<2 cm) or laparoscopic-assisted PCNL (>2 cm) to mobilize bowel under direct vision.

  • Cross-Fused Ectopia

    The ectopic kidney's ureter crosses the midline to its orthotopic bladder orifice—always verify with retrograde pyelography.
