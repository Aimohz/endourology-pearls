1. Ureteral Electrophysiology & Receptor Gradient

  • Pacemaker & Coaptation

    Pacemaker cells in proximal minor calyces drive waves at 2–6 cm/s (2–6/min). Obstruction abolishes luminal coaptation.

  • Adrenergic Density

    α₁D (contraction) and α₁A (tone) receptors peak in the distal third and intramural ureter.

2. Reconciling SUSPEND & Modern Guidelines

  • AUA / EAU Consensus

    SUSPEND's overall negative result was diluted by <5 mm stones (>80% spontaneous passage). For distal ureteral stones 5–10 mm, α₁-blockers raise expulsion by 15%–25%, shorten passage by 3–5 days, and reduce analgesics/ED visits.

3. Passage Benchmarks & The 4–6 Week Ceiling

  • Hard Stop

    Passage rates: <4 mm ~80%, 4–6 mm ~60% (75% MET), 7–10 mm ~35% (50% MET), >10 mm <10%. Never observe past 4–6 weeks (irreversible nephron loss); abort immediately for fever/sepsis, refractory pain, or AKI.
