1. Predictive Nomograms & Triple-D Score

  • Key Cutoffs

    HU < 900–1,000 (>1,000 HU drops SFR <40%) and SSD < 10 cm. Triple-D Score (HU <600, volume <150 mm³, SSD <12 cm): 3/3 criteria yields >90% SFR vs. <35% for 0/3.

2. Repetition Rate & Cavitation Shielding

  • 60–80 vs. 120 Shocks/Min

    Slowing from 120 to 60–80 shocks/min lets cavitation bubbles collapse completely between shocks, preventing acoustic shielding and doubling single-session clearance.

3. Acoustic Coupling & The 2% Bubble Defect

  • Gel Technique

    Air pockets covering just 2% of the coupling cushion reflect 20%–40% of shock energy. Apply a central mound of gel without circular stirring and dock in one smooth vector.

4. Renal Protection: Voltage Ramping

  • Vasoconstrictive Preconditioning

    Deliver initial 100–300 shocks at low voltage before stepping up to induce protective renal vasoconstriction, slashing subcapsular hematoma rates.

5. Absolute Contraindications

  • Non-Negotiable Exclusions

    Pregnancy, uncorrected coagulopathy/active anticoagulation, untreated UTI/sepsis, AAA/renal artery aneurysm > 4 cm in blast path, and distal anatomic obstruction.
