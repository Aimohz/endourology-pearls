1. Positioning & Visceral Shifts

  • GMSV Setup

    Ipsilateral flank elevated 15°–20° with split-leg stirrups. Gravity shifts the colon anteriorly/medially, widening the posterior axillary safety corridor—but the unsupported kidney tents easily, requiring a sharp, deliberate puncture thrust.

2. Cardiorespiratory & Fluidic Advantages

  • Anesthesia & Obesity

    Eliminates 20–30 min prone log-roll, preserves IVC preload and diaphragmatic excursion (preferred in BMI > 40 and COPD/CHF), and maintains immediate airway access.

  • Downhill Drainage

    Horizontal/downward Amplatz sheath drains blood and dust by gravity, keeping IRP < 10–15 cm H₂O.

3. ECIRS Ergonomics vs. Prone Indications

  • When Prone Wins

    Seated surgeon ergonomics & single-position ECIRS favor supine, while prone remains preferred for multi-tract upper-pole supracostal staghorns and severe kyphoscoliosis.
