1. Epidemiology & Mandatory Metabolic Workup

  • High Metabolic Yield

    Incidence rising 5%–10%/yr (adolescent females 15–19 now 120/100,000). >50% harbor a metabolic or anatomic defect—full metabolic workup is mandatory on presentation #1.

2. Sizing & SWL Advantage

  • Instrumentation

    4.5–6.5 Fr semirigid, 7.5 Fr flexible, 11–14 Fr mini-PCNL. Avoid primary UAS in unstented pediatric ureters (pre-stent 1–2 wk first).

  • SWL Excellence

    Single-session SFR >85%–90% due to short SSD and compliant ureters; shield lung bases with an acoustic barrier during upper-pole SWL.

3. Hypothermia & Image Gently Radiation Safety

  • Intraoperative Guards

    Use warmed (37 °C) normal saline at <40 cm H₂O to prevent hypothermia. Pediatric tissues are 10× more radiosensitive: drop fluoro to 1–4 pps and remove the anti-scatter grid in children <20 kg (cuts dose 50%–70%).
