1. Risk Stratification & Kidney-Sparing Selection (Ch. 59)

  • Low-Risk Criteria

    Unifocal, < 1.5–2.0 cm, low-grade cytology & biopsy, and no invasive features on CTU. Also imperative in solitary kidneys, bilateral disease, or CKD.

2. Biopsy Nuances

  • Architecture & Cytology

    Small cup forceps grade accurately (>85%–90%) but understage up to 50%. Perform selective saline barbotage before contrast injection; use tipless flat-wire baskets or 4.5 Fr BIGopsy forceps for intact papillary architecture.

3. Laser Ablation Technique

  • Margin & Wall Safety

    Ho:YAG (0.6–1.0 J, 10–15 Hz) or continuous Tm:YAG (10–20 W). Ablate base + 2–3 mm normal rim; remember the ureteral wall is only 1–2 mm thick.

4. Reverse Thermal Hydrogel & Surveillance

  • UGN-101 / MitoGel

    4 mg/mL MMC gel dissolves over 4–6 h (~58%–60% CR), but causes ureteral stenosis in 25%–40%.

  • Strict Surveillance

    Second-look URS at 6–8 weeks, then q3mo Year 1, q6mo Year 2, and annual URS + CTU thereafter (30%–50% local recurrence).
