1. Dilation vs. Obstruction & MAG3 Limits

  • False-Positive MAG3

    Split renal function < 20%–25% or a huge compliant extrarenal pelvis can produce a flat washout curve (t½ > 20 min) without true mechanical obstruction.

2. Standardized Whitaker Setup & Cutoffs

  • 10 mL/min Challenge

    Antegrade perfusion of warmed dilute contrast at 10 mL/min with simultaneous renal pelvis and bladder manometry (ΔP = P_pelvis − P_bladder).

  • Diagnostic Thresholds

    ΔP < 15 cm H₂O: unobstructed. 15–22 cm H₂O: equivocal (extend infusion or challenge at 15–20 mL/min). > 22 cm H₂O: true mechanical obstruction.

3. Avoiding Artifacts

  • Technical Controls

    Keep bladder drained continuously to gravity, use a double-lumen catheter or ≥18G cannula to avoid cannula-resistance artifacts, and wait for a steady-state hydrodynamic plateau under fluoroscopy.
