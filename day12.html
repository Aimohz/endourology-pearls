1. Encrustation Kinetics

  • Time Thresholds

    <6 weeks: ~10%; 6–12 weeks: ~25%; >12 weeks: 75%–90%. Pregnancy and metabolic stone formers can encase stents in 3–4 weeks.

2. Cardinal Rule: Zero Blind Traction

  • Preventing Avulsion

    Never pull a resistant forgotten stent in clinic—causes stent fracture, ureteral intussusception, or complete avulsion. Always obtain NCCT evaluating distal coil, shaft, and proximal coil (KSA score).

3. Bottom-Up Multimodal Extraction

  • 4-Phase Protocol

    1) Clear bladder anchor via cystolithotripsy. 2) Gentle fluoroscopic mobility test (stop if renal descent sign appears). 3) Semirigid URS alongside shaft with low-power laser. 4) Antegrade PCNL onto proximal coil for K2–K3 burdens.

4. Fractured Stent Rescue & Registries

  • Through-the-Lumen Rescue

    Advance a stiff wire into the fractured stent lumen, inflate a 2.5–3.0 mm angioplasty balloon inside it for friction grip, and withdraw as one unit. Electronic stent registries cut forgotten stents by >95%.
