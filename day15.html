1. Radiation Biology & Occupational Limits

  • ICRP Standards

    Eye lens limit lowered to 20 mSv/yr (posterior subcapsular cataracts); whole body 20 mSv/yr; hands/extremities 500 mSv/yr.

2. C-Arm Geometry & Scatter

  • Tube Below, Detector Above

    Backscatter peaks on the X-ray tube side. Keep the tube under the table and lower the detector as close to the patient as possible.

3. Inverse Square Law & Shielding

  • Distance & PPE

    Doubling distance cuts scatter by 75% (1/d²). Wear 0.5 mm lead apron (>95% block), thyroid collar, and 0.75 mm lead glasses with side-shields (>90% lens protection).

4. Pulsed Fluoro, Collimation & Fluoroless URS

  • Machine & Technique

    Drop continuous 30 pps to 4–8 pps (cuts dose 50%–75%), collimate tightly, use Last-Image-Hold, personally control the pedal, and use direct vision + external shaft markings to achieve <10–15 s fluoro in routine URS.
