1. Primary Hyperparathyroidism & FHH

  • Dual Axis & CCCR

    PHPT accounts for 3%–5% of calcium stones (85% solitary adenoma). PTH drives bone resorption + 1α-hydroxylase calcitriol gut uptake → hypercalciuric spillover. Differentiate from FHH via CCCR < 0.01 (FHH) vs. > 0.02 (PHPT); parathyroidectomy cuts stone recurrence >90%.

2. Distal Renal Tubular Acidosis (Type 1 dRTA)

  • Diagnostic Triad

    Persistent urine pH > 5.5–6.0, hyperchloremic normal-anion-gap acidosis with hypokalemia, and near-zero urinary citrate (< 50–100 mg/d) → carbonate apatite stones & medullary nephrocalcinosis.

3. Randall's Plaques & dRTA Therapy

  • Biomineralization

    Interstitial hydroxyapatite originates in the basement membrane of the thin limbs of the loop of Henle, erodes through papillary urothelium, and anchors idiopathic CaOx stone overgrowth.

  • Pharmacotherapy

    Treat dRTA with potassium citrate 60–120 mEq/d; never use sodium bicarbonate as primary therapy (sodium load worsens hypercalciuria).
