1. Biomaterials & Compression Resistance

  • Polymer vs. Metallic

    Polyurethane offers pushability; silicone improves comfort but is floppy. Metallic coils (Resonance/Memokath) resist >100 mm Hg extrinsic malignant compression and stay indwelling up to 12 months.

2. Hydrodynamics & Sizing

  • Flow Split

    70%–80% of flow is extraluminal in intrinsic stone disease (4.7–6 Fr equals 8 Fr with less pain). Extrinsic obstruction relies 100% on the internal lumen (use 7–8 Fr or tandem/metallic stents).

  • Height-Based Length

    <165 cm: 22–24 cm; 165–178 cm: 24–26 cm; >178 cm: 26–28 cm. Avoid bladder midline crossing by the distal curl.

3. Stent-Related Symptoms & Pharmacotherapy

  • Dual Pain Mechanism

    Trigonal C-fiber irritation + voiding-induced vesicoureteral reflux (40–80 cm H₂O).

  • First-Line Regimen

    α₁-blocker (tamsulosin 0.4 mg / silodosin 8 mg) + NSAID or β₃-agonist (mirabegron 50 mg). Extraction strings (3–5 d) avoid secondary cystoscopy (2%–3% dislodgement risk).
